1. “Like Videos & Earn” Job Scams

Pays a small Rs. 1,000 bonus first to build trust, then steals your savings via “VIP Prepaid Tasks”.

Example: “Earn Rs. 12,000 daily! Like 3 YouTube videos for an instant Rs. 1,500 bonus.”
✓ Rule: Never pay money upfront to unlock tasks or job commissions.
29% of Cases SMS / FB / Instagram

2. Fake SL Post & Giveaway Links

Fake parcel fee SMS or brand prize links ending in .top or .xyz designed to steal card details.

Example: “SL-POST: Parcel held. Pay Rs. 98 fee within 2 hours at slpost-track.top.”
✓ Rule: Official Sri Lankan sites end in .gov.lk, .ac.lk, or .lk.
18% of Cases WhatsApp / Instagram DM

3. 6-Digit OTP & Hacked Friend DMs

A hacked friend’s account asks for a 6-digit SMS code “sent by mistake” or urgent hospital money.

Example: “Malli hadissi! I sent a 6-digit code to your number by mistake, ikmanata ewanna.”
✓ Rule: Never share SMS OTP codes. Always call your friend to verify.
11% of Cases TikTok / Telegram / FB

4. Fake Crypto & Scholarship Scams

Fake trading pages or visa agents promising fast returns or scholarships for an upfront “processing fee”.

Example: “Invest USDT $50 in our AI pool and receive $250 back in 4 hours.”
✓ Rule: Guaranteed fast crypto returns or personal-account visa fees are scams.

Development Programme Mini Project Report Summary

Project Title
Online Scam Awareness Among Sri Lankan Youth: Survey Analysis & Preventive Web Platform
Project Supervisor: Mr. B.D.S.M. Kavisigamuwa
Group Members: K.M.R.I. Suraweera (Developer), K.H. Sumuditha, S.D.T.A. Sumedhaarachchi, S. Thaaniyaluxman
K.M.R.I. Suraweera (Developer) • K.H. Sumuditha • S.D.T.A. Sumedhaarachchi • S. Thaaniyaluxman

1. Executive Summary

Our survey of N = 420 Sri Lankan youth (aged 16–26) found that while social media is used daily, 68.4% struggle to spot fake domains (.top, .xyz) and 81.0% are unaware of how to report scams to Sri Lanka CERT|CC.

2. Key Survey Takeaways (N = 420)

  • 73.1% received WhatsApp/Telegram part-time job scam messages.
  • 42.0% of financial losses came from “VIP Prepaid Task” traps.
  • 68.4% missed fake courier/bank links vs. official .lk sites.
